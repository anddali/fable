When should we scale the ledger worker, and is there a limit?
F

Scale the ledger worker to 5 replicas when the queue depth stays above 10k for more than 5 minutes, and page the on-call if it keeps growing 1. Scaling back down is automatic after 30 minutes below 2k 1.

There is a hard limit: the ledger writer must stay at 1 replica because of the single-writer lock 12. In the August incident, scaling consumers to 8 made lock contention worse rather than better 3.
